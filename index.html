System maps; how I work

    
The same loop, whether the target is a network, a piece of software, or a clinical dataset.

    

      

        
01 Recon

        
Map the surface before touching anything — architecture, dependencies, the assumptions everyone else stopped questioning.

      

      

        
02 Analysis

        
Find where the model of the system and the system itself disagree. That gap is where everything interesting lives.

      

      

        
03 Validation

        
Prove it, don't assume it — reproduce the finding cleanly enough that someone else can verify it without taking my word for it.

      

      

        
04 Reporting

        
Write it up so a non-specialist can understand the risk and a specialist can reproduce the fix. Clarity over cleverness.

      

    

    

      Every engagement runs on written scope, explicit consent, and responsible disclosure. No exceptions — that's what separates this work from a crime.
